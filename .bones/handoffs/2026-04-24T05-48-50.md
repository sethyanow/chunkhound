## Decisions Made

**Architecture target: hybrid local embed + Modal-hosted rerank.** Embeddings are high-frequency (indexing + every query), rerank is low-frequency (search only). Hybrid balances cost and privacy surface — only query + top-K candidate snippets leave the box, never the bulk corpus.

**Model shortlist for pilot:** jina-embeddings-v3 (needs task-param plumbing) and Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (drop-in). Goal: measure both against Voyage baseline on user's private repos before committing. **Rerank target:** Qwen3-Reranker-4B on Modal T4 (not 8B, not L4) — faster cold start outweighs marginal quality at the final ranking stage.
